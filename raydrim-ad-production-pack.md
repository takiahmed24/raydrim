# Raydrim — 30-Second Commercial · Complete Production Pack

Everything needed to produce the ad. Every prompt is standalone — paste as-is,
nothing to assemble.

- **Master format:** 1080 × 1920 (9:16 vertical)
- **Runtime:** 30 seconds (15s cut included)
- **Tool:** Omni Flash — Image mode, then Video → Ingredients
- **Palette:** warm off-white `#f4f1ec` · near-black ink `#16150f` · vermillion `#ff4d2e`

---

## THE THREE RULES

1. **Stills first, always.** Generate all seven images and approve them before
   touching video mode. Stills are permanent and reusable; video renders cost
   credits every time.
2. **Video prompts describe camera motion only.** Never re-describe what's in
   the frame — that's what makes the model redraw and mangle your artwork.
3. **All text is added in the edit.** Nothing readable comes out of the
   generator. Type goes on in CapCut / Premiere / Resolve in Space Grotesk.

### SHOT 1 — from Image 1

```
Animate the supplied source image. This is a finished flat vector
illustration and must be preserved exactly as provided.

Camera: a single slow, steady push in toward the centre of the frame. Scale
increases by approximately 4% in total across the full duration, at a
constant rate with no easing, no acceleration and no overshoot. The camera

Everything in the image is static. No object moves, rotates, scales,
flickers, pulses or animates independently of the camera. There is no
parallax between elements. The illustration behaves as a single flat plane.

Preserve exactly: every shape, line weight, position and proportion in the
source image, and the exact colours — the warm off-white background, the

Do NOT redraw, restyle, re-render or reinterpret any part of the image. Do
NOT add any element not already present — no text, letters, numbers,
particles, sparkles, light rays, shadows, reflections, animated grain or
background motion. Do NOT change the colour palette. Do NOT introduce depth,
3D, perspective shift or camera shake.
```

Freeze Image 5 in the editor. Shots 7 and 8 are your real screenshots with a

### If a render drifts
Shots 2, 6 and 9 are the risky ones — they ask for object animation, which
gives the model licence to redraw. If shapes morph or colours shift, replace
the Camera paragraph with:
```
Camera: completely static and locked off. Nothing in the frame moves at all.
```
A hard cut to a held still reads as deliberate in a Swiss-style ad. A padlock
that melts halfway through does not.

# PART E — AUDIO

- **Music:** sparse, minimal, no vocals. Piano or muted synth pulse, ~90 BPM.
- **Beat sync:** cut shots 5, 6 and 9 on the beat.
- **Ducking:** music down 12 dB under the voiceover.
- **Sound design:** one soft click when the padlock opens (shot 6). One low
  tone as the bars rise (shot 9). Nothing else — the silence is working.
- **Loudness:** −14 LUFS integrated, true peak −1 dB.

---

# PART F — TYPOGRAPHY

- **Face:** Space Grotesk Bold — same as the site.
- **Colour:** `#16150f` on cream. One accent per ad in vermillion `#ff4d2e`:
  the price in shot 9, the URL in shot 10. Nothing else coloured.
- **Position:** lower third, identical placement on all ten shots.
  Consistency reads as design; drifting text reads as amateur.
- **Size:** headline text at ~64px on a 1080-wide frame. Legible at thumbnail.
- **Animation:** fade in over 200ms. No slides, no bounces, no typewriter.
- **Safe area:** keep all text within the centre 80% — social platforms crop
  edges and overlay their own UI at top and bottom.

# PART I — WHAT THIS AD DOES NOT DO

No invented clients. No testimonials. No claimed awards. No stock footage of a
team you don't have. No fabricated dashboards or interfaces. No "trusted by
thousands."

Every claim is checkable in about ninety seconds: the prices are on your
pricing page, the delivery times are on your Whop products, Campus Dude is
live with 22 games, raydrim.com is live and built exactly as described.

For a studio nobody has heard of yet, that verifiability is the entire pitch.
